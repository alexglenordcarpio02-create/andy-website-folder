The Book

PARENTING POINTS

Raising a Happy and Capable Child

Parenting Points helps guide parents through the ups and downs of their child's early development. It offers straightforward advice, understandable explanations and real-life examples of many of the dilemmas commonly faced by parents.

It focuses on how to raise their child using a positive, constructive and sensitive approach—navigating parents through the maze of issues they face when trying to do the right thing for their children.

Meet the Author

About Andy Whitney

Andy has been an early childhood educator for more than 40 years, and at heart, he still sees himself as a teacher who loves working with young children and the people who guide them.

Originally from the United States, Andy's career has taken him around the world—living and working in Washington, D.C., Bethesda, Maryland, Indonesia, and Singapore.

Andy earned his degree in Child Development from the University of Maine and later completed a Master's in Education at Northeastern University.
