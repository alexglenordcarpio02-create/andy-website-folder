PARENTING POINTS

Raising a Happy and Capable Child

Parenting Points helps guide parents through the ups and downs of their child's early development. It offers straightforward advice, understandable explanations and real-life examples of many of the dilemmas commonly faced by parents. It focuses on how to raise their child using a positive, constructive and sensitive approach.

From defining developmental expectations to providing advice on behavior management to outlining the importance of positive change that supports their child, Parenting Points navigates parents through the maze of issues that they often face when trying to do the right thing for their children.

About Andy Whitney

Andy has been an early childhood educator for more than 40 years, and at heart, he still sees himself as a teacher who loves working with young children and the people who guide them. Originally from the United States, Andy's career has taken him around the world—living and working in Washington, D.C., Bethesda, Maryland, Indonesia, and Singapore.

Over the years, he has taught in early childhood classrooms, worked as a head teacher, spoken to aspiring teachers, and provided seminars and workshops to parents of young children.

Andy earned his degree in Child Development from the University of Maine and later completed a Master's in Education at Northeastern University. But he'll tell you that some of his greatest lessons have come from the children, parents, and teachers he has had the privilege of knowing throughout his career—people who have taught him something at every stage of life.
